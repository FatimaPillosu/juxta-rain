Processing (changes made to the ECMWF data)

  • 24 h totals for IFS Control and AIFS Single are differences of the total precipitation accumulated from the start of the run: tp(T+L) − tp(T+L−24). IFS tp (m) is converted to mm; AIFS tp is already in kg m⁻² (mm). Small negative differences from data packing (down to about −0.1 mm) are set to 0.
  • Each 24 h window is aligned with the run's cycle: 00/12 UTC runs give windows ending at 00 or 12 UTC; 06/18 UTC runs give windows ending at 06 or 18 UTC.
  • Fields are re-encoded as Cloud-Optimised GeoTIFFs on the original grids: amounts rounded to 0.1 mm, probabilities to 1 %. No interpolation or smoothing; zoomed-out views sample grid points (nearest neighbour). Values at a point are those of the nearest grid point of each model.
  • The ecPoint charts are downloaded as vector PDFs and only their map area is shown (title and legend removed), otherwise as published, with a link to the original chart.
  • At the chosen point, the ecPoint row of the table gives the colour class of the chart there (for example 40.5–60.5 %), not a probability value. It is read from the chart's own coloured polygons, redrawn without coastlines or gridlines; on the charts checked it matches the displayed colour on more than 99.9 % of the map. The probability chart at the point shows the same class range as a shaded band for every 24 h window. ecPoint is not used in any score.

Coastlines and land boundaries: Natural Earth 1:50m (public domain), coordinates rounded to 0.01°.

What this site is not

It is not a forecasting or warning service. Its purpose is to show the difference between gridded forecasts and point observations, and what it is reasonable to expect from each. For warnings, use your national meteorological service (in Europe, Meteoalarm).
